**VLC media player** is a free and open-source multimedia player developed by the non-profit project VideoLAN. For more than 20 years it has been the default answer to one simple question: how do I play this file?

## Why VLC stands out

- Plays virtually every video and audio format out of the box — no codec packs needed
- Completely free: no ads, no tracking, no premium version
- Runs on Linux, Windows, macOS, Android and iOS
- Can convert files, record your screen and stream over a network
- Supports subtitles, playlists and hardware acceleration

## Who it is for

Everyone. If a file refuses to open in another player, VLC will almost certainly open it. That single feature made it one of the most downloaded open-source applications in history.

## Where to get it

VLC is available on the official website videolan.org, in the repositories of most Linux distributions, and in the app stores of every major platform.

*Know a better player? Most people end up keeping VLC anyway — it simply works.*
